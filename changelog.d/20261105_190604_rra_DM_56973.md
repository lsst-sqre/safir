### Backwards-incompatible changes

- Switch from HTTPX to HTTPX2 for all Safir interfaces. HTTPX is no longer being publicly maintained and HTTPX2 appears to be the preferred replacement. See the [HTTPX2 transition guide](https://pydantic.dev/docs/httpx2/get-started/migration/) for details on how to switch. respx users should use pytest-httpx2 for appropriate fixtures and may need some additional configuration; see the [Safir documentation](https://safir.lsst.io/user-guide/http-client.html#testing-considerations) for more information.
